# Long-running agents

I use my plasma physics research as a test bed for AI agents that work on hard problems for weeks. Physics suits the test because many of its answers can be checked. The agent's rules, its checks and its mistakes are all in a public repo, [krmhd-research](https://github.com/anjor/krmhd-research).

The posts below are grouped by what they teach. For the physics itself, see the [research page](../research/index.md).
