# Talks & Events

I speak about two things. The first is how Forward Deployed Engineering actually works: how to structure the org, how to train FDEs, and how the role is changing under AI. The second is what AI changes about doing scientific research.

If you'd like me to speak to your team or portfolio, get in touch via [Platypus Technologies](https://platypustech.xyz).

## 2026

- **Scaling Forward Deployed Engineering: Services to Productisation**: Earlybird VC Gradient Descending Summit (November 2026). Moderated a panel with Notion and Wonderful, for a room of early-stage AI founders. Notion grew on self-serve and is now adding FDEs. Wonderful grew by putting engineers inside its customers, and now has to turn that work into product.
- **If LEGO Had Forward Deployed Engineers**: Summit Partners AI Retreat (October 2026). A talk for portfolio CEOs on what FDE is, whether you need it and how to start.
- **Return to Phase Space with an AI Assistant**: 17th Plasma Kinetics Working Meeting, Wolfgang Pauli Institute, Vienna (August 2026). A talk and working-week contribution on rebuilding a gyrokinetics code with AI, validating the result, and what becomes possible when AI handles more of the computational research loop. [Reflection →](../writing/posts/a-week-in-vienna.md) · [Programme →](https://www-thphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/research/plasma/wpi/workshop2026.html)
